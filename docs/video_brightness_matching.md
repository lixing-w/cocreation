# Video edit brightness matching

Video edits match the first clip in their edit lineage by default. For `v1 -> v2 -> v3`, both v2 and v3 match v1; v3 still receives the corrected v2 as its model input. Lineage follows executable `references`, not the historical `edit_depth` field. Copy operations preserve lineage. Independent/reference generation and concatenation begin a new chain. Legacy source-shot selections stay attached to the anchor.

A video edit must have exactly one base-video reference; ambiguous edits fail validation before inference. New shot/reference generation may legitimately have several independent video design references and receives no brightness anchor. Pure planner inspection of 120 seeds (0–119, dessert observation, one reserved rework) found no multi-parent video edits, but seed 22 generated a new shot from two separate video design references (person and studio table).

The matcher samples 24 normalized clip times at reduced resolution, finds stable spatial tiles using structure/chroma agreement, and rejects changed silhouettes. It jointly fits equally weighted shadow, midtone and highlight quantiles, with a weaker whole-stable-region constraint. Weighted monotone regression produces one clip-wide luma LUT; legal black/white and out-of-range values remain fixed. U/V are not adjusted. The same curve applies to every frame. Independent alternating samples must improve the balanced tone error without a material regression in any tone band.

The method is automatic and does not use the dessert example's manually selected sweater/pants regions. It can skip clips with insufficient stable content, incompatible framing/color encoding, unsupported HDR/bit depth, or no validated improvement. Skips preserve the model output and record the reason. Flattened black pixels have no recoverable texture; a tone curve cannot reconstruct that detail. Stable-region selection is heuristic and does not guarantee identical content correspondence after a large scene change.

Matching re-encodes the video at CRF 16 while retaining geometry, presentation timestamps and audio packets. Native-output mode continues to preserve the model's dimensions and duration; edited outputs now receive the optional luma adjustment. Disable matching with `materialize.py --no-match-video-brightness` or `materialize(..., match_video_brightness=False)`. The actual-prompts launcher defaults `MATCH_VIDEO_BRIGHTNESS=1`; set it to 0 to opt out.

`--dry-run` and `media_manifest.json` expose `video_brightness_matching` with the planned root references. Each edit record has `brightness_matching` containing anchor reference/hash, status/reason, stable coverage, held-out tone errors and the applied LUT. Matching policy/root hashes participate in edit fingerprints. Turning matching on during resume reuses unchanged independent generation roots but invalidates edits and affected dependents. Existing runs are not modified unless materialization is explicitly resumed.
